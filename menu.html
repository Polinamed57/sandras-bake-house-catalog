Sandra's Bake House About us

Big cookies, small batches.

Sandra's Bake House makes big, soft, NYC-style cookies for Edmonton cafés and coffee shops. Every batch is mixed, scooped and baked by hand in a licensed commercial kitchen that follows all food safety standards.

Each cookie weighs around 110–120 g, so it looks generous in your display case and feels like a real treat next to a latte.

Ready to sell

Individually wrapped and labelled with full ingredients. Straight from the box to your counter.

Less waste

Delivered fresh-frozen. Thaw only what you need, keep the rest in the freezer for up to 2 months.

Proudly local

Small-batch and made right here in Edmonton. A local story your customers will love.

Unwrap & warm me up.A quick warm-up brings back that soft, fresh-from-the-oven feel. Your staff get simple handling and storage instructions with every order.
